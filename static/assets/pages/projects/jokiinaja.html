Jokiin Aja is a web-based service marketplace that enables users to offer and purchase freelance services through a centralized platform. Similar to Fiverr, the platform allows service providers to create service listings, showcase their expertise, and manage customer orders, while clients can browse available services, place orders, and track project progress. The system was developed to simplify service transactions, improve accessibility between clients and providers, and create opportunities for individuals to monetize their skills through a secure and user-friendly platform.
