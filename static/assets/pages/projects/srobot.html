Adventure in Pasar




Srobot is a 3D platformer adventure game where players control a small robot on a mission to repair a damaged spaceship. To complete the mission, players must explore various environments, collect scattered ship components, and overcome challenging obstacles. Along the way, players can earn points by collecting items and successfully navigating hazardous areas while progressing toward restoring the spaceship.

Development

  • Game Engine: Unity
  • Programming Language: C#
  • Platform: PC
  • Role: Game Programmer
  • Features:
    • 3D platforming and obstacle navigation
    • Collectible spaceship repair parts
    • Score collection system
    • Time Freeze ability
    • Time Slow ability
