Adventure in Pasar




Adventure in Pasar is a top-down adventure game where players explore a lively traditional market to recover stolen goods. Navigate through crowded market alleys, interact with merchants, complete objectives, and overcome various challenges while uncovering the mystery behind the missing items. The game combines exploration, quest-solving, and light action mechanics in a vibrant market-themed environment inspired by local culture.

Development

  • Game Engine: Unity
  • Programming Language: C#
  • Platform: PC
  • Role: Game Programmer
  • Features:
    • Top-down character movement system
    • NPC interaction and dialogue system
    • Quest and mission progression
    • Inventory and item collection mechanics
    • Traditional market environment exploration
    • Objective-based gameplay and storytelling

Project Highlights

  • Developed gameplay systems for a market-themed adventure experience.
  • Implemented player interaction and quest progression.
  • Created immersive exploration gameplay inspired by Indonesian traditional markets.
  • Contributed to designing engaging objectives that encourage players to explore and interact with the environment.
  • Focused on creating a fun and accessible experience while showcasing local cultural elements.
