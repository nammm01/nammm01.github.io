Bordir24



Link HKI

Bordir24 is an online embroidery ordering platform designed to simplify the process of custom embroidery requests. Customers can browse available services, submit design requirements, place orders, and monitor the progress of their embroidery projects through a user-friendly web interface. The platform aims to make embroidery services more accessible, efficient, and convenient for both customers and business owners.

Key Features

  • Online custom embroidery ordering system
  • Product and service catalog
  • Order management and tracking
  • Customer account management
  • Responsive design for desktop and mobile devices
  • Easy-to-use interface for submitting embroidery requests

Development

  • Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
  • Frameworks & Libraries: Bootstrap, jQuery
  • Backend: PHP (Laravel)
  • Database: MySQL
